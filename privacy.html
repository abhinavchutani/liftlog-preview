LiftLog

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

LiftLog is a personal, independently-run strength-training logbook. This page explains what data the app touches and what happens to it, in plain language.

What's collected

  • If you use Demo mode: nothing leaves your device. Your logged workouts, name, and preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it permanently — there is no copy anywhere else.
  • If you create an account (Real mode): your email address and password are sent to Supabase (our database and authentication provider) to create your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text — Supabase hashes them. Workouts you log (name, date, duration, and total volume) are stored in a Supabase Postgres database tied to your account.

Who can see your data

Your workout rows are protected by database-level security rules (Row Level Security) that only let your own signed-in account read or write them. The app developer can access the underlying Supabase project as its administrator, but does not routinely read individual accounts' data.

Third parties

Two services are involved in running LiftLog:

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles authentication. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website's static files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

There is no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDK in this app.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual logged workouts yourself from the app (each session has a delete button). To delete your entire account and all associated data, email the address below.

Changes to this policy

This is a small, actively-developed personal project. This policy may change as features are added (for example, if payments are ever introduced). Material changes will update the date at the top of this page.
